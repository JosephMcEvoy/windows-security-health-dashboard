# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.
- Show empathy. Someone filing a confused bug report is usually someone having
  a bad day with their fleet.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages — and when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **joe.maci@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this policy,
and may ban contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate.
